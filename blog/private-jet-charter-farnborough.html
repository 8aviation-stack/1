Private Jet Charter from Farnborough Airport

A private jet Farnborough departure combines award-winning ground service with easy access to west London, Surrey and the Thames Valley. This guide explains why Farnborough is the premier business airport to the south-west of the capital, and how to fly from it.

Farnborough Airport occupies a singular place in British aviation. The birthplace of powered flight in the United Kingdom, it is now one of Europe's most respected dedicated business airports, purpose-built to serve private and corporate travellers with a level of polish that few can match. For anyone based to the west or south-west of London, it is often the most sensible place to begin a journey by private jet charter.

Why Farnborough is the premier business airport

Unlike the capital's larger hubs, Farnborough exists solely for business aviation. There are no airline schedules, no mass-market terminals and no compromise on service. The result is an environment designed entirely around efficiency and discretion. Its modern terminal, consistently recognised in the industry for the quality of its facilities, sets the tone from the moment you arrive.

Situated in Hampshire near the M3 corridor, the airport is ideally placed for Surrey, Berkshire and the Thames Valley, as well as affluent towns such as Guildford, Windsor, Ascot and Weybridge. For travellers in these areas, Farnborough frequently proves quicker to reach than crossing London to an airport on the far side. Handling and the terminal

Farnborough's reputation rests heavily on its ground handling. Passengers are received in a calm, contemporary lounge, with private security and, for international trips, dedicated customs and immigration. Formalities are brief and unobtrusive, and most guests move from car to cabin in a matter of minutes.

8Aviation manages every operational detail on your behalf, from slot coordination and fuel to bespoke catering and crew briefing. Access and getting there

  • By road: The airport sits close to the M3, giving straightforward access from west and south-west London, Surrey and the Thames Valley, often without touching the capital's worst congestion.
  • By chauffeur: Our chauffeur service delivers you directly to the terminal entrance, with the same care extended at your arrival airport abroad.
  • For events: Farnborough is a natural choice for Ascot, Goodwood, Silverstone and the golf and equestrian calendar of the south of England.

Popular routes from Farnborough

Farnborough's clientele travels widely, but a handful of routes recur again and again. The Alpine and Mediterranean corridors are perennial favourites, whether that means a winter run to Geneva for the ski season, a summer hop to Nice for the Riviera, or an island escape to Ibiza. Business travellers regularly connect to Paris, Zurich, Milan and Frankfurt, while the airport's longer-range capability supports non-stop travel to New York and the Middle East. Aircraft available from Farnborough

The airport comfortably accommodates the entire range of business aircraft, so the right choice comes down to your route, party size and priorities:

  • Turboprops — the King Air 250 and Pilatus PC-12 for efficient shorter sectors.
  • Light jets — the Citation CJ1, M2, CJ2+ and Embraer Phenom 300E for quick European trips.
  • Midsize jets — the Citation XLS+ and Sovereign+ for greater range and cabin comfort.
  • Ultra-long-range jets — our specialism, including the Gulfstream G650, Bombardier Global 7500 and Global 6000, for intercontinental journeys without a fuel stop.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Farnborough Airport?+

Farnborough Airport lies in Hampshire, to the south-west of London, close to the M3 corridor. It is especially convenient for west and south-west London, Surrey, the Thames Valley and towns such as Guildford, Windsor and Ascot.

Why choose Farnborough over other London airports?+

Farnborough is a purpose-built business airport with award-winning handling and no scheduled airline traffic. Travellers based to the west of the capital often find it faster and calmer than crossing London, and its facilities are among the finest in Europe.

What aircraft operate from Farnborough?+

Farnborough handles the full spectrum of business aircraft, from light jets such as the Citation CJ2+ and Phenom 300E through midsize Citation XLS+ and Sovereign+ to ultra-long-range types including the Gulfstream G650 and Global 7500 for non-stop intercontinental travel.
