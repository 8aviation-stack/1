Bombardier Global 7500: The Longest-Range Business Jet

The Bombardier Global 7500 is the longest-range business jet in service, and for a Global 7500 charter it pairs that reach with a cabin arranged into four distinct living zones — a true flagship for ultra-long-haul travel.

When the mission is the very longest kind, the Global 7500 is in a class of its own. Ultra-long-range travel is 8Aviation's specialism, and across twenty years of arranging private jet charter we have watched this aircraft redefine what a single non-stop sector can achieve. This guide explains its range, its remarkable cabin and why so many clients ask for it by name.

The longest range in business aviation

The Global 7500's headline quality is reach. With an approximate range of around 7,700 nautical miles, it is the longest-range purpose-built business jet in service, opening up very long non-stop sectors that few other aircraft can attempt with a full cabin. From London it connects effortlessly to New York, Dubai and much further afield, flying over rather than around, and turning what were once two-leg journeys into a single, uninterrupted flight.

Four living zones

Range is only half the story. What sets the Global 7500 apart inside is space — enough to divide the cabin into four distinct living zones. Depending on the aircraft, these can include a dedicated crew and galley area, a club or conference space for working and meetings, a dining or entertainment zone, and a private stateroom for genuine full-flat rest. On a very long sector this changes everything: a delegation can hold a meeting up front while others dine or sleep, arriving prepared rather than exhausted.

The cabin is quiet and pressurised to a low cabin altitude, and its ride quality is widely regarded as exceptional. Large windows and considered lighting complete an interior designed for people who spend serious hours in the air.

Space for the whole party

The Global 7500 comfortably carries a full long-haul party with abundant room for luggage and equipment, and full-length beds for overnight travel. It is the natural choice when a group needs both the range to fly non-stop and the space to work and rest in separate areas. To see how it complements our other aircraft, explore the full aircraft fleet.

Why charter the Global 7500

Clients choose the Global 7500 when nothing less will do: the longest journeys, the largest cabins, and the ability to treat the flight itself as productive, comfortable time. It is the aircraft for a board that must arrive ready to negotiate, or a family who want the journey to feel like part of the destination. Its natural stablemate in our fleet is the Gulfstream G650 — read our Gulfstream G650 guide to compare the two flagships. You can also see ultra-long-range aircraft at work on our London to New York and London to Dubai routes.

Typical routes from London

The Global 7500's range makes almost every intercontinental route from London a non-stop proposition — New York, Dubai, the US West Coast, the Gulf and much of Asia among them. Indicative flight times such as around seven and a half hours to New York and around seven hours to Dubai give a sense of its ease on the routes we fly most, though actual durations always depend on winds, routing and conditions on the day.

G650 or Global 7500?

Clients often ask us to help them choose between the two flagships, and the honest answer is that both are superb. The Gulfstream G650 is prized for its fast, high cruise and beautifully quiet cabin, making light of routes such as London to New York. The Global 7500 answers a slightly different brief: when the priority is the very longest sector or the largest, most flexibly divided cabin, its class-leading range and four living zones give it the edge. In practice we match the aircraft to the mission — the number of passengers, the length of the sector, the balance of work and rest you need — and we are always happy to talk it through.
