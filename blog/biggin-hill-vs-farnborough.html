Biggin Hill vs Farnborough: Which London Airport for Your Private Jet?

Both are outstanding dedicated business airports, so the Biggin Hill vs Farnborough question is rarely about which is better in the abstract. It is about which is better for you. Here is an honest comparison to help you decide.

When you charter a private jet London departure, two airports dominate the shortlist: Biggin Hill to the south-east of the capital and Farnborough to the south-west. Both are purpose-built for private and business aviation, both are free of scheduled airline traffic, and both can handle everything from a light jet to an ultra-long-range Gulfstream. The deciding factors are more practical than prestige, and they come down to geography, access and the character of each airport.

Location and access

Biggin Hill sits roughly 14 miles south-east of central London, which makes it the closer of the two in absolute terms and especially convenient for the City, Canary Wharf and south-east London. Farnborough lies further out in Hampshire near the M3, but for anyone in west London, Surrey, Berkshire or the Thames Valley it is frequently the faster option because it avoids crossing the capital entirely.

The honest truth is that drive time, not distance, is what matters. A principal in Mayfair and a family in Weybridge may reasonably choose different airports for the same flight. Our door-to-door chauffeur service is available from either, so the ground experience is equally seamless whichever you pick.

Handling and facilities

Both airports offer the hallmarks of first-class business aviation: private lounges, dedicated security, discreet customs and immigration, and ground teams that move you from car to cabin in minutes. Farnborough is widely celebrated for the polish of its modern terminal, while Biggin Hill pairs efficient, personable handling with a genuine sense of aviation heritage. In practice, both deliver the calm, queue-free departure that defines private travel.

Does the airport affect the price?

Airport choice has a relatively modest effect on the overall cost compared with the aircraft type and the route flown. Handling and landing fees differ slightly between the two, but these are usually a small share of a charter. The far larger drivers are cabin size and distance, which is why we always recommend choosing the airport that minimises your ground time and letting us optimise the rest. Frequently asked questions

Is Biggin Hill or Farnborough closer to central London?+

Biggin Hill is closer to central London in absolute terms, roughly 14 miles south-east, and is well placed for the City and Canary Wharf. Farnborough sits further out to the south-west but is often quicker for those based in west London, Surrey and the Thames Valley.

Which airport is better for private jet charter?+

Neither is objectively better; the right choice depends on where you are travelling from. The decisive factor is usually your home or office location and the resulting drive time, since both airports offer excellent handling and can accommodate the same aircraft.

Can both airports handle long-range jets?+

Yes. Both Biggin Hill and Farnborough can operate ultra-long-range aircraft such as the Gulfstream G650 and Bombardier Global 7500, making non-stop flights to New York, Dubai and beyond possible from either airport.
