Private Jet Flight Times from Farnborough

Farnborough is one of Europe's finest business-aviation airports, purpose-built for private travel to London's west and the Home Counties. Here are the approximate private jet flight times from Farnborough to the destinations our clients request most.

Set in Hampshire, roughly thirty-five miles south-west of central London, Farnborough serves only private and business flights. Its dedicated terminal, swift handling and generous operating hours make it a favourite for travellers based in west London, Surrey, Berkshire and beyond. As at any private airport, the airborne time is only half the story; the real gain is on the ground. If chartering is new to you, our guide on how to charter a private jet walks through every step.

How Farnborough compares with Biggin Hill

Because Farnborough lies west of London and Biggin Hill to the south-east, airborne times to European destinations are broadly similar, usually within a few minutes of each other. The right choice comes down to where your journey begins on the ground. Farnborough suits travellers to the west and south-west; Biggin Hill favours those in south and east London and Kent. Our comparison of Biggin Hill versus Farnborough weighs both in detail.

Why private is faster door-to-door

At Farnborough there is no scheduled terminal, no bag drop and no security queue. You are met kerbside, walk a short distance to the aircraft and depart. On arrival your luggage is off the aircraft within minutes and a car is waiting on the apron. Across a typical European trip this saves two to three hours each way compared with a scheduled airline, and with our door-to-door chauffeur service the whole journey becomes one seamless movement. For the full picture, see private jet versus first class.

Planning your schedule

Farnborough's efficiency lets you plan around your day rather than an airline's timetable. We suggest arriving around fifteen minutes before your slot; there is no bag drop to queue for and no gate to reach. A same-day return from Geneva or Milan is straightforward, and a Friday-evening dash to the Riviera fits neatly after the working week. As ever, winds and seasonal routing can move airborne times by a few minutes, so we confirm the expected figure when you book.

Matching the aircraft to the route

Short sectors to Paris or Zurich are ideal for a light jet, Mediterranean runs to Nice or Ibiza suit a midsize cabin, and transatlantic flights to New York require an ultra-long-range aircraft, a particular strength of ours.
