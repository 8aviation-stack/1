Private Jet from London to New York

A private jet to New York is one of the most rewarding journeys we fly. With the right ultra-long-range aircraft, London to New York is a comfortable non-stop crossing of roughly seven and a half hours, delivering you to Teterboro rested and ready.

For two decades, 8Aviation has arranged transatlantic private jet charter for business leaders, families and private travellers. The London–New York corridor is among the busiest premium routes in the world, and it is precisely the sort of long-haul mission where our ultra-long-range specialism comes into its own. Where a scheduled first-class ticket ties you to fixed times and crowded terminals, a private charter is built entirely around your schedule.

How long does it take?

On an ultra-long-range business jet such as a Gulfstream G650 or a Bombardier Global 7500, London to New York is a genuine non-stop flight. Westbound, against the prevailing jet stream, you should plan for roughly 7 hours 30 to 7 hours 45 minutes in the air, with the return leg often a little quicker thanks to favourable winds. These are indicative times; actual flight duration depends on routing, weather and air-traffic conditions on the day.

Crucially, an ultra-long-range aircraft makes the crossing with a full cabin and generous baggage without needing a technical fuel stop. That non-stop capability is the difference between arriving relaxed and arriving weary, and it is why we so often recommend a heavy or ultra-long-range jet for this route.

The time-zone advantage

New York sits five hours behind London. Depart Biggin Hill mid-morning and you can land at Teterboro in the early afternoon, local time, with a full working day still ahead of you. For business travellers this is transformative: a meeting in Manhattan, dinner in the city, and a return the following day is entirely achievable. Because you set the departure time, you can shape the itinerary around your commitments rather than around an airline timetable.

Where you land: Teterboro and beyond

Teterboro is the preferred business-aviation gateway for Manhattan, just a short drive from Midtown. Its dedicated private terminals mean you step from the aircraft to a waiting chauffeur in minutes, with none of the queues associated with the major international airports. Depending on your final destination we can also arrange arrivals at Westchester, Republic or the principal New York airports. Our door-to-door chauffeur service operates on both sides of the Atlantic, so a car meets you at the aircraft steps in both London and New York.

Which aircraft suits the route?

The choice of aircraft shapes the whole experience. Both of our flagship long-range types cross the Atlantic in supreme comfort, with full-flat beds, generous cabin height and the quiet, low-altitude cabin pressurisation that leaves you fresher on arrival. To understand the differences, read our Gulfstream G650 guide and our Bombardier Global 7500 guide. Both are perfectly at home on London–New York, and our advisers will match the cabin to your party size, baggage and preferences.

  • Gulfstream G650 — a long-range flagship with a fast, high cruise and a beautifully quiet cabin.
  • Global 7500 — the longest-range business jet in service, with four distinct living zones.
  • Both comfortably seat a full transatlantic party with room for luggage and equipment.

Business or leisure

The route serves two very different travellers equally well. For the executive, it is about time reclaimed: no connections, no lost days, and the ability to work or rest in complete privacy at 41,000 feet. For families and leisure travellers, it is about ease and discretion — arriving in New York without the friction of a crowded terminal, then continuing seamlessly to the Hamptons, New England or onward across the United States. To see how our fleet spans these needs, explore the full aircraft fleet.

Departing from London

We fly the route from all of London's principal business-aviation airports. Biggin Hill is a firm favourite for South and Central London, offering swift private handling and easy road access. Farnborough, Luton and Stansted are all available depending on where you are travelling from. Indicative pricing for London–New York varies with aircraft type and season, and every trip is quoted individually — please ask us for a firm figure.

Frequently asked questions

How long is a private jet flight from London to New York?+

On an ultra-long-range aircraft such as a Gulfstream G650 or Global 7500, London to New York is comfortably non-stop, typically around 7 hours 30 to 7 hours 45 minutes depending on winds and routing.

Which New York airport can a private jet use?+

Teterboro is the preferred business-aviation gateway for Manhattan, but private flights can also use Westchester, Republic and the main international airports. We advise on the fastest option for your final destination.

Do I need an ultra-long-range jet for London to New York?+

It is the most comfortable way to fly the route non-stop with a full cabin and generous baggage. Smaller jets can make the crossing but may require a technical stop, so we usually recommend a heavy or ultra-long-range aircraft.
