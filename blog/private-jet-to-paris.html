Private Jet from London to Paris

A private jet to Paris is one of the quickest, most civilised hops in Europe — around an hour in the air, into Le Bourget, and minutes from a meeting or a long lunch in the city. Here is how the route works, for business and leisure alike.

London to Paris is the archetypal short private-jet hop: two great capitals, barely an hour apart, connected dozens of times a day. For a board meeting, a client dinner, fashion week or simply a spontaneous weekend, private jet charter transforms the journey. Instead of the friction of a scheduled flight or a train with fixed departures, you arrive at a private terminal minutes before take-off, fly directly into Paris's dedicated business airport, and step into a waiting car. The whole point is time — and on this route the time saving is dramatic.

8Aviation has flown the London–Paris corridor for over twenty years. Our 24/7 team can arrange an aircraft at short notice, hold it for your return, and coordinate chauffeurs at both ends so the day runs to your schedule, not the airline's.

Flight time from London to Paris

Plan for a flight time of approximately 1 hour 5 minutes from Biggin Hill to Paris — one of the shortest sectors we fly. As always, the exact figure moves with aircraft type, winds and air traffic. On so brief a hop, almost all the advantage of flying private is on the ground: no early check-in, no security queues, no waiting at a gate. You are wheels-up shortly after you arrive.

Paris Le Bourget — and why fly private

Paris Le Bourget (LFPB) is Europe's premier business aviation airport, dedicated entirely to private flights and located just to the north-east of the city — considerably closer to central Paris than the main commercial hubs. Flying private into Le Bourget means a swift, discreet arrival: a short taxi to the stand, a walk of moments through a private terminal, and straight into your car. For a business traveller with an appointment in the 8th arrondissement or La Défense, it is difficult to imagine a more efficient way to reach the city.

The best aircraft for the route

On so short a sector, a light jet is usually the ideal choice — efficient, quick to turn around, and perfectly comfortable for a one-hour flight with four to six passengers. Aircraft such as a Phenom 300 or Citation are natural fits. Where the party is larger, or the trip forms one leg of a longer itinerary, a midsize cabin adds space and luggage capacity. Our fleet covers every category, and because we operate, manage and broker aircraft, we can put the right jet on the ramp quickly, even at short notice. If you are comparing this with a scheduled first-class ticket, our piece on private jet vs first class sets out the real differences.

Business, leisure and the same-day return

The London–Paris route is the definition of a business jet workhorse — and its greatest strength is the same-day return. Fly over for morning meetings, hold the aircraft through the day, and be home for dinner in London, all without an overnight stay. Because we can keep your jet and crew on standby at Le Bourget, your return departs precisely when your day finishes. For leisure travellers the same ease applies: a weekend in Paris begins the moment you leave home, with no travel day lost at either end. It is one reason the route features so prominently among the most popular private jet destinations we fly, and a textbook case of private jet for business travel.

Ground transfer at both ends

A seamless day depends on the cars as much as the jet. As standard, 8Aviation arranges chauffeur-driven transfers at both ends — collecting you from your London address for the short run to Biggin Hill, and meeting you on the apron at Le Bourget to carry you into the city. On the return, the process reverses seamlessly. One point of contact manages the entire itinerary, so a trip to Paris feels less like travel and more like stepping between rooms.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the flight from London to Paris by private jet?+

Allow approximately 1 hour 5 minutes from London Biggin Hill to Paris. The exact time depends on aircraft type, winds and air traffic, but it is one of the quickest and most popular private jet hops from London.

Which airport do private jets use for Paris?+

Paris Le Bourget (LFPB) is Europe's premier business aviation airport, close to central Paris and dedicated entirely to private flights. It offers a fast, discreet arrival and short transfers into the city.

Can I fly to Paris and back in a day by private jet?+

Yes. The short flight time and quick terminals make a same-day return to Paris straightforward, which is why the route is so popular for business meetings. We can hold your aircraft for the return leg to suit your schedule.
