Flying Private from London Biggin Hill: The Complete Guide

For discerning travellers, a private jet Biggin Hill departure is the quickest, calmest way out of the capital. Here is everything you need to know about London's dedicated business-aviation airport, from the FBO experience to the aircraft that fly from it.

London Biggin Hill Airport has earned its reputation as the capital's dedicated business-aviation gateway. Unencumbered by scheduled commercial traffic, it exists to serve one kind of traveller: those who value their time. If you are weighing where to begin your next journey, this guide sets out why so many principals, families and corporate teams choose to fly from here, and how 8Aviation arranges the whole experience around you.

Why Biggin Hill?

Biggin Hill lies roughly 14 miles south-east of central London, tucked into the green edge of the capital yet remarkably close to the West End and the City. Its enduring appeal is simple. Because it handles no airline schedules, there are no crowds, no snaking security lines and no departure-board anxiety. The airport is built entirely around private and business flights, which means the pace on the ground is unhurried and the emphasis is on discretion.

The airport also holds a genuine sense of history, having played a celebrated role in the defence of London, and today that heritage sits alongside some of the most modern private-aviation facilities in Europe. For anyone comparing options, our overview of private jet charter from Biggin Hill covers the practical detail in depth, while this guide focuses on the experience itself.

The experience on the day

Flying private rewrites the choreography of air travel. Rather than arriving hours ahead, most guests reach Biggin Hill around 15 minutes before departure. A chauffeur, arranged as part of a door-to-door service across Europe and the USA, can deliver you directly to the FBO entrance. From there, formalities are brief and handled privately, often while you enjoy a coffee in the lounge.

There is no shared terminal, no gate and no boarding scramble. When your aircraft is ready, you walk a short distance to the steps and settle in. For many first-time private flyers, it is this contrast that leaves the strongest impression: the sheer absence of friction. Handling and the FBO

The FBO, or fixed-base operator, is the private terminal where your journey begins and ends. Biggin Hill is known for fast, attentive handling: dedicated lounges, private customs and immigration, and ground teams who coordinate everything from baggage to catering without fuss. Arrivals are equally seamless, with passengers frequently in their onward car within minutes of the aircraft door opening.

Behind the scenes, 8Aviation coordinates slots, fuel, catering and crew so that the day unfolds exactly as planned. Whether your aircraft is one we operate, manage or carefully broker, our 24/7 operations team is the single point of contact throughout.

Access to London

  • By car: Central London is typically 30 to 45 minutes away, with the City and Canary Wharf especially convenient from the airport's south-eastern position.
  • By helicopter: A short transfer to the London heliport at Battersea can place you beside the Thames within minutes, ideal when the diary is unforgiving.
  • By chauffeur: Our door-to-door chauffeur service means the transition from home or office to cabin is entirely handled for you.

Who flies from Biggin Hill?

The airport suits a broad mix of travellers. Corporate teams value the proximity to the City and the ability to reach a European meeting and return within a single day. Families appreciate the calm, private environment and the flexibility to travel on their own schedule. And for long-haul travellers heading to New York, the Middle East or beyond, Biggin Hill's runway comfortably accommodates the ultra-long-range jets that make those journeys non-stop. Aircraft available from Biggin Hill

One of the airport's strengths is its versatility. The full range of private aircraft can operate from here, and choosing the right one is a balance of range, passenger numbers and budget:

  • Turboprops — the King Air 250 and Pilatus PC-12 are efficient choices for short domestic and near-European hops.
  • Light jets — the Citation CJ1, M2, CJ2+ and Embraer Phenom 300E suit quick continental trips for smaller groups.
  • Midsize jets — the Citation XLS+ and Sovereign+ add cabin space and range for longer European routes.
  • Ultra-long-range jets — a specialism of ours, the Gulfstream G650, Bombardier Global 7500 and Global 6000, and Challenger 605/Global Express XRS reach New York, Dubai and further without a fuel stop.

Frequently asked questions

How far is Biggin Hill from central London?+

London Biggin Hill sits roughly 14 miles south-east of central London. By car it is typically around 30 to 45 minutes to the West End or the City, and a helicopter transfer to the London heliport can be arranged in a matter of minutes.

How early do I need to arrive at Biggin Hill for a private flight?+

Most passengers arrive around 15 minutes before departure. Because the FBO handles a small number of guests at a time, there are no public queues and formalities are brief, so check-in feels closer to arriving at a private lounge than a terminal.

What aircraft can fly from Biggin Hill?+

Biggin Hill accommodates everything from turboprops such as the King Air 250 and Pilatus PC-12 through light and midsize jets to ultra-long-range aircraft including the Gulfstream G650 and Bombardier Global 7500, which can reach New York, Dubai and beyond non-stop.
