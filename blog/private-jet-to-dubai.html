Private Jet from London to Dubai

A private jet to Dubai links two of the world's great business and leisure capitals in a single, comfortable hop. On an ultra-long-range aircraft the journey is non-stop in around seven hours, delivering you to the Gulf rested and ready to go.

Dubai is one of the most requested long-haul destinations we serve, and it plays directly to 8Aviation's ultra-long-range specialism. For twenty years we have arranged private jet charter between London and the Gulf for corporate teams, family offices and private travellers who value discretion and time. Whether the purpose is a boardroom in the DIFC or a winter break by the Palm, a private charter shapes the entire journey around you.

How long does it take?

On an ultra-long-range business jet, London to Dubai is a genuine non-stop flight of roughly seven hours. These are indicative times; the exact duration depends on routing, winds and air-traffic conditions on the day. The key point is that a heavy or ultra-long-range aircraft makes the crossing with a full cabin and generous baggage — no technical fuel stop, no interruption to your rest or work.

With the United Arab Emirates four hours ahead of London, timing works elegantly in both directions. An overnight departure lets you sleep across the cabin and arrive in Dubai for breakfast, ready for a full day; an evening return brings you home to London the same night.

Where you land: DXB and Al Maktoum

Two airports serve the emirate for private aviation. Dubai International (DXB) offers a central location close to the city and the financial district, with dedicated executive handling that whisks you from aircraft to car in minutes. Al Maktoum (DWC) at Dubai World Central is the alternative, well placed for the newer developments and for larger aircraft movements. Our advisers will recommend the best fit for your schedule and final destination, and our door-to-door chauffeur service can meet you at the steps in both London and Dubai.

Which aircraft suits the route?

London–Dubai is a signature ultra-long-range mission, and our flagship types are perfectly suited to it. Both offer full-flat beds, a quiet cabin and the low cabin altitude that leaves you fresher on arrival — invaluable when a meeting or event follows soon after landing. To weigh the options, read our Gulfstream G650 guide and our Bombardier Global 7500 guide.

  • Gulfstream G650 — a fast, high-flying flagship that makes light of the seven-hour sector.
  • Global 7500 — the longest-range business jet in service, with four separate living zones for work, dining and rest.
  • Both carry a full party non-stop with ample room for luggage and equipment.

Business and luxury in equal measure

Few routes blend commerce and leisure quite like this one. For business, Dubai is a global hub for finance, trade and events, and a private charter means arriving composed and on your own timetable, with the privacy to prepare en route. For luxury travellers, it is the gateway to the region's finest hotels, beaches and experiences — and onward across the Gulf, to the Maldives or to Asia. To see the range of cabins we can offer, explore our full aircraft fleet.

What to expect on board

A seven-hour sector is the perfect length to make the most of a private cabin. On our ultra-long-range aircraft you can hold a working meeting after take-off, dine at a time that suits you, and then convert the cabin to full-flat beds for a proper sleep — arriving in Dubai genuinely rested rather than merely transported. Catering is arranged to your preferences, connectivity keeps you in touch throughout, and the crew are briefed on the small details that matter to you. For a group travelling together, the ability to work, dine and rest in separate parts of the cabin is one of the quiet luxuries of flying private on a route of this length.

Onward from the Gulf

Dubai is also a natural staging point for journeys further east. Many clients pair a stay in the emirate with onward travel to the Maldives, the Seychelles or across Asia, all of which sit comfortably within reach of an aircraft already positioned in the region. Because we can hold the same aircraft and crew for a multi-leg itinerary, a single enquiry can cover the entire trip, with our team coordinating slots, handling and chauffeurs at every stop.

Departing from London

We fly the Gulf route from all of London's principal business-aviation airports. Biggin Hill is a popular choice for South and Central London, with swift private handling and straightforward road access, while Farnborough, Luton and Stansted are all available. Indicative pricing for London–Dubai depends on aircraft type and season, and every journey is quoted individually — please ask us for a firm figure.

Frequently asked questions

How long is a private jet flight from London to Dubai?+

On an ultra-long-range aircraft the flight is comfortably non-stop, typically around seven hours depending on winds and routing. Times are indicative and vary with weather and air-traffic conditions.

Which Dubai airport do private jets use?+

Private flights commonly use Dubai International (DXB) for its central location, or Al Maktoum (DWC) at Dubai World Central. We advise on the best option for your schedule and final destination.

Is a non-stop private jet to Dubai possible from London?+

Yes. Ultra-long-range jets such as the Gulfstream G650 and Global 7500 fly London to Dubai non-stop with a full cabin and baggage, with no technical stop required.
