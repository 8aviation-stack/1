The Most Popular Private Jet Destinations from London

Some routes come up again and again. From Riviera weekends to transatlantic boardrooms, these are the most popular private jet destinations from London, with approximate flight times and a note on the aircraft that suits each.

London's business-aviation airports open the door to the whole of Europe within a couple of hours and to the world's great cities in a single hop. Below are ten destinations our clients return to year after year. All flight times are approximate and subject to aircraft type, winds and routing. To see how the times stack up route by route, our guides to flight times from Biggin Hill and flight times from Farnborough lay them out in full.

Nice · ~1h 50

The gateway to the Cote d'Azur and Monaco, Nice is arguably the most requested leisure route from London. Summer sees a steady stream of midsize jets heading south for Cap Ferrat, Antibes and the Grand Prix. See our dedicated guide to a private jet to Nice.

Ibiza · ~2h 20

The Balearic island is a summer institution, and private charter turns a long-planned trip into a spontaneous one. A midsize or super-midsize jet handles the sector comfortably with room for luggage. More detail in our private jet to Ibiza guide.

Geneva · ~1h 35

Business hub in every season and ski gateway in winter, Geneva is one of the busiest private routes from London. From here the Alpine resorts of Verbier, Courchevel and Megeve are a short transfer away. See our private jet to Geneva guide.

Paris · ~1h 05

The classic short hop. A private jet from London to Paris is airborne for barely an hour, and with quick clearance at both ends it is genuinely a same-morning business trip. Read more in our private jet to Paris guide.

Milan · ~1h 45

Italy's business and fashion capital draws executives and design houses alike, while Lake Como and the Italian lakes are within easy reach for a longer weekend. A midsize jet is the natural fit for the route.

Palma · ~2h 20

Mallorca's capital pairs a glamorous marina with easy access to the island's coves and countryside. Like Ibiza, it is a summer favourite best served by a midsize or super-midsize cabin with generous baggage space.

Zurich · ~1h 30

Switzerland's financial centre is a reliable year-round business route and a springboard to the eastern Alps. Fast, efficient and close to London by air, it suits a light or midsize jet depending on party size.

New York · ~7h 45

The definitive transatlantic route, flown non-stop by an ultra-long-range aircraft such as a Gulfstream G650 or Global 7500, a specialism of ours. Arrive rested, on your own schedule. See our private jet to New York guide.

Dubai · ~7h 00

A leading route for business and luxury travel to the Gulf, Dubai is reached non-stop by a heavy or ultra-long-range jet. A flat bed and a productive cabin make the sector pass easily. More in our private jet to Dubai guide.

Cannes · ~1h 55

Synonymous with the film festival and the yachting season, Cannes-Mandelieu brings you closer still to the western Riviera than Nice. A midsize jet is ideal, with a short chauffeured transfer to the Croisette.

Which aircraft for which destination?

European destinations are comfortably reached by light and midsize jets, while transcontinental trips to New York and Dubai call for heavy or ultra-long-range aircraft flown non-stop.
