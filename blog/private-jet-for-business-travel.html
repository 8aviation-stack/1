Private Jet Charter for Business & Corporate Travel

For companies where senior people's time is the scarcest resource, private jet business travel is less a luxury than a productivity tool. Here is how chartering turns lost hours into working time, unlocks multi-city days, and keeps your team sharp — with London Biggin Hill as one of the fastest gateways to the capital.

The case for chartering a private jet on business rarely rests on comfort alone. It rests on time, control and the ability to do things a scheduled airline simply cannot accommodate — three board meetings in three cities before dinner, a discreet cabin in which to finalise a deal, a departure moved by two hours because negotiations ran long. For organisations that fly with intent, the aircraft becomes an extension of the office.

The time saving is the real return

Airline travel hides its cost in the margins: the early arrival, the security queue, the connection, the airport that is an hour from where you actually need to be. Private charter compresses all of that. You arrive at a private terminal minutes before departure, walk straight to the aircraft, and fly directly to the airport nearest your meeting — often one that airlines do not serve. Across a return trip, the saving is routinely measured in hours, and those hours belong to your most senior and most expensive people.

Multi-city days and roadshows

Nothing showcases the advantage like a multi-city itinerary. Because the aircraft stays with your team, a private jet can carry a management roadshow through two or three cities in a single day and have everyone home the same evening. Investor roadshows, site visits, client tours and due-diligence trips all become dramatically more efficient when the schedule bends to you rather than the other way round. Our most popular private jet destinations guide illustrates how quickly the European financial centres connect.

Productivity in the cabin

A private cabin is a secure, uninterrupted working environment. Teams can review confidential documents, rehearse a pitch or hold a candid strategy discussion with no risk of being overheard — something impossible in a shared airline cabin. Equally, the flight can be downtime to arrive rested and prepared. Either way, the journey is productive rather than dead time. For a fuller comparison with premium airline cabins, see private jet charter versus first class.

Matching the aircraft to the mission

Business travel spans everything from a two-person day trip to a full board flying intercontinental. Our fleet is built for that range. A light jet such as the Phenom 300E or Citation CJ2+ is ideal for European hops; a midsize Citation XLS+ or Sovereign+ adds cabin space and range for longer roadshows; and for transatlantic board travel or reaching the Gulf non-stop, ultra-long-range aircraft such as the Gulfstream G650 and Bombardier Global 7500 carry a full team in comfort. You can view the options on our fleet page, and our guide on choosing the right private jet size helps match cabin to trip.

Corporate accounts and 24/7 booking

Companies that fly frequently benefit from a corporate account: streamlined booking, consistent service standards, a single point of contact and consolidated billing. Because our charter desk operates around the clock, a schedule that changes at short notice — as business schedules do — is handled without drama. With twenty years' experience and operated, managed and carefully brokered aircraft to draw on, we can respond to last-minute and same-day requests as readily as trips planned in advance. If you are new to the process, our step-by-step guide to how to charter a private jet sets out exactly what to expect.

Why Biggin Hill is a corporate advantage

Location matters, and for business travellers to and from the capital, London Biggin Hill is hard to beat. As a dedicated business-aviation airport it offers swift handling, no scheduled-airline congestion, and a genuinely fast onward link into central London — by chauffeur or, when minutes count, by helicopter. Add our door-to-door chauffeur service across Europe and the USA and the office-to-meeting journey becomes seamless. That efficiency is the essence of private jet charter for business.

Frequently asked questions

How does a private jet save time on business travel?+

You arrive minutes before departure at a private terminal, skip queues and connections, fly directly to airports nearer your destination, and can visit several cities in a single day. The saving is measured in hours per trip, not minutes.

Can a private jet visit multiple cities in one day?+

Yes. Multi-city itineraries and roadshows are a core strength of private charter. The aircraft stays with your team throughout, so you can hold meetings in two or three cities and be home the same evening.

Do you offer corporate accounts for regular flyers?+

Yes. For companies that fly frequently we can set up a corporate account with streamlined booking, consistent service and consolidated billing. Contact charter@charter-private-jet.com to discuss your travel patterns.

Why is Biggin Hill good for business travel to London?+

London Biggin Hill is a dedicated business-aviation airport with swift handling and a fast onward link into central London, including by helicopter and chauffeur, making it one of the quickest gateways for corporate travellers.
