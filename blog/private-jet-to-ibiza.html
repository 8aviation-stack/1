Private Jet to Ibiza: Flight Times, Costs & Tips

A private jet to Ibiza turns a long summer travel day into a two-hour hop. Here are the flight times, the aircraft that suit the route, why booking early matters, and how we handle everything from London doorstep to island villa.

Ibiza has become one of the most sought-after private jet destinations in the Mediterranean — and for good reason. The island packs world-class beach clubs, calm northern coves, superyacht harbours and hilltop villas into a short, warm summer. It is also one of the most congested places to reach on a scheduled ticket at peak season, which is precisely why private jet charter makes such a difference here. From London Biggin Hill you can leave in the morning and be poolside by lunch, with no connections, no crowds and no compromise on timing.

8Aviation has flown clients to the Balearics for two decades. Our 24/7 team manages the whole journey — aircraft, slots, handling and chauffeur — so your only decision is when to leave.

Flight time from London to Ibiza

Plan for a flight time of approximately 2 hours 20 minutes from Biggin Hill to Ibiza. As with any charter, the real figure moves with aircraft type, winds and traffic on the day, so treat it as a realistic guide. What you gain over scheduled travel is at the airport, not in the air: a private terminal, a few minutes from car to cabin, and departure on your schedule rather than the airline's.

Ibiza airport — and why fly private

Ibiza (LEIB) is the island's single airport, on its southern side within easy reach of Ibiza Town, Playa d'en Bossa and the villa districts to the north. In July and August it is one of the busiest leisure airports in Europe, and the contrast between the commercial terminal and the private aviation experience is stark. Flying private, you avoid the queues, arrive minutes before departure, and step off the aircraft into a waiting car — the difference between starting your holiday relaxed and starting it exhausted.

The best aircraft for Ibiza

For the London–Ibiza sector, a midsize jet is usually the ideal choice. At a little over two hours in the air, a stand-up cabin makes the journey noticeably more comfortable, and the larger baggage hold easily swallows a group's luggage — no small consideration for a beach holiday. A midsize aircraft such as a Citation XLS or Hawker comfortably carries six to eight passengers, while a super-midsize adds range and cabin space for larger parties or those continuing elsewhere in the Mediterranean. Light jets remain a nimble, cost-effective option for smaller groups travelling light. Our fleet covers every category, and because we operate, manage and broker aircraft, we recommend the right one for your party rather than simply what is nearest. If you are unsure, our note on how to choose private jet size will help.

When to go — and why to book early

Ibiza's season is short and intense. From June to September the island fills, with weekends the busiest of all as Friday departures and Sunday returns concentrate demand. That pressure runs right through the supply chain: aircraft, crew, handling and airport slots all tighten, and last-minute requests can be difficult to satisfy at any price. The single best piece of advice for a summer trip is therefore to book early. Securing your aircraft weeks ahead protects your preferred departure times and generally represents better value. Shoulder months — late May and September — offer warm seas, a calmer island and easier availability for those with flexibility. Departing from the uncongested apron at Biggin Hill is itself a way of sidestepping the summer crush at London's larger airports.
