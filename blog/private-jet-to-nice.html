Private Jet to Nice & the Côte d'Azur

A private jet to Nice puts the whole of the French Riviera within easy reach of London — Cannes, Monaco, Antibes and St-Tropez are all a short transfer from the runway. Here is how the route works, which aircraft suits it, and how to arrive in style.

Few routes suit private aviation as neatly as London to the Côte d'Azur. The Riviera is compact, glamorous and time-sensitive — a lunch in Monaco, a regatta in St-Tropez or a film premiere in Cannes rewards travellers who arrive fresh and leave on their own schedule. With private jet charter from London Biggin Hill, you swap the queues and connections of scheduled travel for a discreet terminal, a short walk to the aircraft, and a door-to-door journey managed entirely by one team.

8Aviation has arranged flights to the South of France for more than twenty years. Whether you are travelling for the summer season, a board meeting on the coast or a private celebration, our 24/7 team can pair the right aircraft to your party and have a chauffeur waiting on the apron in Nice.

Flight time from London to Nice

Expect a flight time of approximately 1 hour 50 minutes from Biggin Hill to Nice Côte d'Azur. The precise figure depends on aircraft type, winds and air traffic on the day, so treat it as a realistic guide rather than a fixed timetable. The real advantage is not raw speed but the time you save on the ground: arrive around fifteen minutes before departure, clear a private terminal in moments, and step straight onto your jet.

Nice Côte d'Azur airport — and why fly private

Nice Côte d'Azur (LFMN) is the third-busiest airport in France and the natural gateway to the Riviera, with dedicated private aviation terminals separate from the main commercial concourse. Flying private here means a calm, unhurried arrival: no baggage carousels, no crowds, and a swift transfer to your onward transport. The airport sits right on the Mediterranean, moments from the Promenade des Anglais, and is superbly placed for the entire coastline.

From Nice, the geography works in your favour. Monaco is a short drive or a scenic helicopter hop along the coast; Cannes and Antibes lie to the west; and St-Tropez is reachable by car or, in peak season, by air. Cannes–Mandelieu is an alternative for smaller aircraft close to the Croisette, though runway length limits the jets it can accept — our team will advise on the best field for your group.

The best aircraft for the Riviera

For most parties travelling from London, a light or midsize jet is the sweet spot. Light jets such as a Phenom 300 or Citation are efficient and quick to turn around for four to six passengers with cabin bags. Step up to a midsize cabin — a Citation XLS or similar — and you gain a stand-up cabin, greater luggage capacity for a summer's worth of trunks, and more comfort across the two-hour sector. Larger groups, or those combining Nice with onward legs, may prefer a super-midsize aircraft. Our fleet spans every category, and because we operate, manage and broker aircraft, we match the machine to your journey rather than the other way round. When to go and who flies there

The Riviera runs to a calendar. Late spring brings the Cannes Film Festival and the Monaco Grand Prix within days of one another, and demand for aircraft and slots peaks accordingly — early booking is essential. Summer, from June to September, is the classic season: yacht owners, families and business travellers all converge on the coast, and weekends see the heaviest traffic. Autumn offers quieter skies and warm seas for those with flexibility. Whatever the occasion, flying from Biggin Hill keeps you clear of the congestion at London's larger hubs.

Ground transfer on arrival

A private jet is only half the journey. As standard, 8Aviation arranges a chauffeur-driven car to meet you on the apron and carry you door-to-door — to your hotel in Cap-Ferrat, your villa above Cannes or the harbour in Monaco. For the coast's more remote corners, or to skip the summer traffic on the Basse Corniche, a helicopter transfer can be arranged from Nice. Everything is coordinated by a single point of contact, so your itinerary flows seamlessly from London doorstep to Riviera terrace.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the flight from London to Nice by private jet?+

Allow approximately 1 hour 50 minutes from London Biggin Hill to Nice Côte d'Azur. The exact time varies with aircraft type, headwinds and air traffic, but a private charter is typically quicker end-to-end than a scheduled flight once airport time is removed.

Which airport do private jets use for the Côte d'Azur?+

Nice Côte d'Azur (LFMN) is the principal gateway, with dedicated private aviation terminals. It is the ideal base for Cannes, Monaco, Antibes and St-Tropez, with helicopter and chauffeur transfers available on arrival.

What size jet is best for a trip to Nice?+

A light or midsize jet suits most parties travelling from London to Nice. Light jets are efficient for four to six passengers, while a midsize cabin adds comfort, luggage capacity and a stand-up cabin for larger groups or longer stays.
