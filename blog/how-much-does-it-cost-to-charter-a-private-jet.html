How Much Does It Cost to Charter a Private Jet?

Private jet charter cost is one of the first questions every client asks — and the honest answer is that it depends. Below we explain what actually drives the price, share indicative hourly ranges by aircraft category, and set out why every 8Aviation quote is fixed and tailored to your journey.

There is no single price for chartering a private jet, because no two trips are the same. A short hop across the Channel in a light jet and a non-stop crossing to New York aboard an ultra-long-range aircraft sit at opposite ends of the scale. What unites them is a shared set of cost drivers. Understand these and you will understand your quote — and be able to shape a trip that delivers the experience you want at a price that makes sense.

What drives private jet charter cost

Six factors do most of the work in determining what you pay. They interact, which is why a fixed, tailored quote will always be more accurate than any rule of thumb.

1. Aircraft category

This is the single biggest lever. A turboprop such as the King Air 250 or Pilatus PC-12 costs a fraction of an ultra-long-range Gulfstream G650 to operate. Larger cabins burn more fuel, require more maintenance and carry more crew. Choosing the right category for your mission — rather than the largest aircraft available — is the most effective way to control cost. Our guide to choosing the right private jet size walks through the trade-offs in detail.

2. Flight time

Charter is priced largely by the hour the aircraft flies, so a longer route naturally costs more. Flight time also governs crew duty and fuel, and it determines whether a trip can be flown non-stop or needs a fuel stop. You can compare typical durations in our overviews of private jet flight times from Biggin Hill.

3. Positioning and empty legs

An aircraft rarely starts the day exactly where you need it. If it must fly empty to reach your departure airport — a positioning flight — that time forms part of the cost. The flip side is the empty leg: a repositioning flight an operator would otherwise fly empty, offered at a discount when the route and date happen to match your plans.

4. Airport and handling fees

Landing charges, handling, parking and passenger fees vary considerably between airports. Dedicated business-aviation airports such as London Biggin Hill and Farnborough are efficient and swift, while some primary hubs carry premium slot and handling costs. Where you fly from and to therefore influences the total.

5. Crew

Type-rated captains and first officers, and cabin crew on larger aircraft, are costed into every charter. Trips that keep crew away overnight, or that exceed a single duty day, add crew accommodation and duty considerations to the quote.

6. Passenger numbers and route pattern

The number of passengers can push you up a cabin category, and multi-leg or multi-day itineraries introduce repositioning and waiting time. A well-planned single-aircraft business itinerary is often more economical than it first appears, because the jet stays with you rather than being re-chartered at each stop.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to charter a private jet?+

It depends chiefly on aircraft category and flight time. As an indication, hourly rates run from around £1,800–£2,500 for a turboprop, £2,500–£3,500 for a light jet, £3,500–£5,000 for a midsize jet, and £8,000–£12,000 or more for an ultra-long-range aircraft. Every 8Aviation quote is fixed and tailored to your route, dates and passenger numbers.

Why is a private jet quoted as a fixed price rather than per hour?+

Hourly rates are a useful guide, but the real price of a trip also reflects positioning flights, airport and handling fees, crew duty, overnights and passenger numbers. We combine all of these into a single fixed, all-in price so you know the total before you confirm.

What is an empty leg and can it save money?+

An empty leg is a repositioning flight an aircraft must fly without passengers. When your date and route align with one, it can be chartered at a substantial discount. Availability is limited and short-notice, so flexibility is key.

Are there costs beyond the flight itself?+

An 8Aviation quote is all-inclusive of the aircraft, crew, fuel, standard handling and catering. Optional extras such as door-to-door chauffeur transfers, premium catering or winter de-icing are confirmed clearly in advance.
