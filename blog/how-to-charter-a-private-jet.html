How to Charter a Private Jet: A Step-by-Step Guide

If you have never done it before, chartering a private jet can seem opaque. In reality it is remarkably simple. Here is exactly how to charter a private jet with 8Aviation, from your first enquiry to stepping off the aircraft — five clear steps, and a 24/7 team handling the detail.

Booking a scheduled airline seat and chartering a private jet feel like different worlds, but the process of arranging a charter is refreshingly straightforward. You are not navigating fare classes or fixed timetables; you are telling an experienced team where you want to be and when, and letting them build the flight around you. Below we set out the five steps, the information worth having to hand, and how our round-the-clock desk turns an enquiry into a confirmed flight.

The five steps to chartering a private jet

Step 1 — Enquire

Everything begins with a short message. Tell us your departure and destination, your preferred dates and times, and how many passengers are travelling. You can enquire online through our quote request, or email the desk directly at charter@charter-private-jet.com. There is no obligation and no charge for a quote — this first step simply opens the conversation.

Step 2 — Receive tailored options

Our charter team reviews aircraft availability and returns a curated set of options, each with a fixed, all-in price. Rather than a single take-it-or-leave-it aircraft, you typically see a small range — perhaps a cost-effective light jet, a roomier midsize cabin, and an aircraft with the legs for a non-stop routing. We draw on operated, managed and carefully brokered aircraft, so the shortlist genuinely fits your mission. If you are unsure which cabin suits you, our guide to choosing the right private jet size is a useful companion, and you can view every aircraft on our fleet page.

Step 3 — Confirm

Once you have chosen, we confirm the booking, issue the charter agreement and finalise the details: passenger names for security and manifest, catering preferences, and any ground transport. This is the point at which the flight becomes firm and the aircraft and crew are reserved for you. Payment terms are set out clearly and the price you confirmed is the price you pay.

Step 4 — Fly

On the day, there is no long check-in and no crowded terminal. Arrive at the private terminal — at London Biggin Hill, Farnborough, Luton or Stansted — typically around fifteen minutes before departure, walk a few steps to the aircraft, and take off close to your scheduled time. Your crew know your preferences before you board. For a fuller picture of the Biggin Hill experience, see flying private from Biggin Hill.

Step 5 — Arrive

On landing you are moments from the exit and your onward car. Where you have arranged it, our door-to-door chauffeur service across Europe and the USA is waiting on the apron. The whole point of private travel is that arrival is as unhurried and seamless as departure.

What information to give us

The more you can tell us up front, the sharper the options we can return. It helps to have the following to hand:

  • Departure and destination airports or cities
  • Preferred dates and times, and any flexibility around them
  • Number of passengers, and any children or pets
  • Luggage of note — golf clubs, skis, oversized items
  • Cabin preferences, catering requirements and dietary needs
  • Whether you need chauffeur transfers at either end

None of this is essential to get started — a simple "four of us, London to Nice, next Friday" is enough for a first quote — but detail sharpens the result. If you are still comparing routes, our most popular private jet destinations guide is a good place to gather ideas.

How the 8Aviation 24/7 desk works

Private travel rarely keeps office hours, so neither do we. Our charter desk operates around the clock, every day of the year, which means an enquiry at midnight is handled with the same care as one at midday. With twenty years' experience and a fleet spanning turboprops to the ultra-long-range Gulfstream G650 and Bombardier Global 7500, we can respond to same-day and short-notice requests as readily as trips planned weeks ahead. One point of contact manages your booking end to end — aircraft, crew, catering and transfers — so you make a single call and everything follows.

That combination of experience, fleet breadth and a genuinely 24-hour team is what makes private jet charter London feel effortless. When you are ready, tell us your journey and we will take it from there. To understand what shapes the price you are quoted, read how much it costs to charter a private jet.

Frequently asked questions

How far in advance should I book a private jet?+

Ideally a few days to a week ahead to secure the best aircraft and pricing, but our 24/7 desk regularly arranges flights at very short notice — often within a few hours where an aircraft and crew are available.

What information do I need to provide to get a quote?+

Your departure and destination airports or cities, preferred dates and times, the number of passengers, and any specific requirements such as cabin size, catering or chauffeur transfers. The more detail you give, the more precise the options we can return.

How quickly will I receive charter options?+

Our team typically responds promptly with tailored aircraft options and a fixed price. For urgent or same-day travel we prioritise your enquiry and can often confirm within the hour.

Do I need to arrange my own transfers and catering?+

No. We can arrange door-to-door chauffeur transfers across Europe and the USA, bespoke catering and any special requests as part of your booking, so your entire journey is handled end to end.
