Private Jet to Geneva for the Ski Season

A private jet to Geneva is the fast, seamless way to reach the Alps — with the slopes of Verbier, Courchevel and Megève all within a scenic transfer of the runway. Here is how the route works through the winter season.

When the snow arrives, Geneva becomes one of the busiest private aviation gateways in Europe — and the reason is simple. No other airport puts so many world-class ski resorts within a short transfer. For skiers heading to the French and Swiss Alps, private jet charter from London removes the two most tiresome parts of a winter trip: the congested scheduled flights over the holidays, and the long, uncertain onward journey. Leave Biggin Hill after breakfast and you can be clicking into your bindings by mid-afternoon.

8Aviation has flown clients to the Alps every winter for more than twenty years. Our 24/7 team handles aircraft, slots, de-icing, handling and ground transfer as one coordinated package, so a family, a group of friends or a corporate party arrives together and on time.

Flight time from London to Geneva

Allow approximately 1 hour 35 minutes from Biggin Hill to Geneva. The precise time shifts with aircraft type, winter winds and air traffic, so treat it as a realistic guide. In peak season, the ground experience is where private travel truly earns its keep: no holiday-crowd queues, a private terminal, and departure aligned to your resort transfer rather than a fixed airline schedule.

Geneva airport — and why fly private

Geneva (LSGG) sits right on the Franco-Swiss border, moments from the city and perfectly placed for the Alps. It has well-established private aviation facilities, and flying private means you skip the long winter queues that build at the commercial terminal over the holidays. You arrive minutes before departure, clear a calm private lounge, and transfer straight to your car or helicopter. In a season when the scheduled airport can be overwhelmed, that difference is the whole point.

The best aircraft for the Alps

A light or midsize jet handles the London–Geneva sector comfortably. Light jets are efficient and quick for smaller parties, while a midsize cabin adds room, a stand-up interior and — importantly for a ski trip — generous baggage capacity for skis, boots and boards. Because winter operations bring cold-weather considerations such as de-icing and mountain-airport performance, aircraft selection and crew experience matter. Our fleet spans every category, and as an operator that also manages and brokers aircraft, we match the machine to both your group and the conditions. If you are weighing cabin sizes, our note on how to choose private jet size is a helpful reference.

When to go and who flies there

The ski season runs broadly from December to March, and demand is heavily concentrated. The Christmas and New Year fortnight, and February half-term, are the peaks — aircraft, crew and airport slots are all at their tightest, and early booking is essential to secure your preferred times. January and the back half of March often bring excellent snow with noticeably easier availability. Families, groups of friends and corporate teams all favour the route, drawn by the ease of arriving together and dropping straight into resort. Departing from Biggin Hill keeps you clear of the winter congestion at London's larger hubs.

Ground transfer to the resorts

Geneva's great advantage is what lies beyond the runway. Verbier, Courchevel, Megève, Chamonix and Val d'Isère are all within reach by road, and as standard we arrange a chauffeur-driven car — often a comfortable 4x4 for mountain roads — to meet you on arrival and carry you door-to-door to your chalet. For the highest or most distant resorts, or to rise above the traffic on a busy transfer day, a helicopter can be arranged directly from the airport. Everything is coordinated by a single point of contact, so the journey flows seamlessly from London to the piste.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the flight from London to Geneva by private jet?+

Allow approximately 1 hour 35 minutes from London Biggin Hill to Geneva. The exact time depends on aircraft type, winds and air traffic, so treat it as a realistic guide rather than a fixed figure.

Which resorts is Geneva the gateway to?+

Geneva is the principal gateway to the Alps, with road transfers to Verbier, Courchevel, Megève, Chamonix, Val d'Isère and beyond. Chauffeur or helicopter transfers can be arranged directly from the airport.

When is the busiest time to fly to Geneva?+

The ski season from December to March, and especially the Christmas, New Year and February half-term weeks, is the busiest period. Aircraft and airport slots are in high demand, so early booking is strongly advised.
