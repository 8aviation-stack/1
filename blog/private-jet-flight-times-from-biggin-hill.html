Private Jet Flight Times from Biggin Hill

London Biggin Hill sits on the doorstep of the capital, and its private terminal turns the airport formalities into minutes rather than hours. Below are the approximate private jet flight times from Biggin Hill to the destinations our clients ask for most.

Biggin Hill is one of London's dedicated business-aviation airports, roughly fourteen miles south-east of the City. There are no scheduled airlines here, only private movements, which is precisely why departure and arrival are so quick. For most European trips you can leave your home or office and be airborne well inside the time it would take simply to clear security at a scheduled terminal. Why private is faster door-to-door

Look only at the airborne figures and a private jet and an airliner appear broadly similar over the same route. The difference is everything that happens on the ground. At Biggin Hill there is no terminal to trek through, no bag drop, no security queue and no gate. You are met kerbside, walk a few paces to the aircraft, and take off. On arrival your bags come off within minutes and a car is waiting on the apron.

Across a typical European trip that ground efficiency saves two to three hours each way. Add our door-to-door chauffeur service at both ends and the journey becomes a single seamless movement rather than a series of queues. For a fuller comparison, see private jet versus first class.

Choosing the right aircraft for the distance

Shorter hops to Paris or Zurich are comfortably flown by a light jet, while Mediterranean runs to Nice or Ibiza suit a midsize cabin with a little more range and space. Transatlantic sectors such as New York call for an ultra-long-range aircraft, a specialism of ours. You can browse the categories on our fleet page, and our guide to choosing the right private jet size matches cabin to mission.

Planning your schedule

Because departure is so quick, you can build a tighter itinerary than any airline timetable allows. A morning meeting in Geneva and an afternoon back in London is entirely realistic, as is a Friday-evening departure to Nice once the working week is done. We recommend arriving at the terminal around fifteen minutes before your slot; there is no need for the two-hour buffer a scheduled flight demands. Winds and seasonal routing can nudge airborne times either way, so we confirm the expected figure at the time of booking.

Biggin Hill as your London gateway

With fast clearance, generous operating hours and quick access to south London, Surrey and Kent, Biggin Hill is an ideal base for both business and leisure travel. Frequently asked questions

How long is a private jet flight from Biggin Hill to Paris?+

Approximately 1 hour 5 minutes in the air. As with every route, the figure varies with aircraft type, winds and air-traffic routing on the day.

Can I fly from Biggin Hill to New York non-stop?+

Yes. With an ultra-long-range aircraft such as a Gulfstream G650 or Global 7500, Biggin Hill to New York is roughly 7 hours 45 minutes non-stop, subject to payload and headwinds.

Are private jet flight times faster than airline schedules?+

The airborne time is similar, but the total journey is far shorter. Private terminals mean you arrive minutes before departure and skip queues, connections and long transfers, typically saving two to three hours per trip.
