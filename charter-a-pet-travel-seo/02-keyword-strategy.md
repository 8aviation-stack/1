# Keyword Strategy — Pet Flights by Private Jet (charter-a.com)

**Date:** 28 August 2026

## Honest caveat first
No public dataset reports *conversion rate per keyword* for this niche — that data only exists in your own Google Ads / GA4 account once the page runs. What follows is ranked by **commercial intent + SERP evidence** (which terms the pages that make money all target), which is the best available proxy. If you run even a small Google Ads test on the shortlist below, its conversion data should override this ranking.

## Primary keyword (recommended)

### `pet friendly private jet charter`
**Why it converts best:**
- **"Charter" is the buying word.** A searcher typing *charter* is specifying the product they intend to purchase — unlike *pet flight* / *pet travel*, which split between owners researching airline cargo, pet couriers, and general info.
- **The whole market titles on it.** VistaJet (VistaPet), K9 Jets, Mercury Jets, Global Charter, Atlas Jet, Charter Wind and Air Charter Advisors all lead their money pages with this exact phrase. That convergence is years of competitor conversion data speaking.
- It naturally contains the sub-phrases *pet friendly private jet* and *private jet charter*, so one page covers the head and the modifier variants.

**On-page targets:** title tag, H1, first 100 words, one H2, image alt, meta description, internal anchor text from other pages.
