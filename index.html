Questions

Private jet charter, answered.

How much does it cost to charter a private jet from Biggin Hill?+

The cost of a private jet charter depends on the aircraft, the route, flight time and the number of passengers — a light jet for a short European hop costs far less than an ultra-long-range flight to the USA. Every 8Aviation quote is fixed and tailored to your journey. Send your requirement and our 24-hour team will respond promptly with options and a price.

Can I charter a private jet from Farnborough, Luton or Stansted?+

Yes. Biggin Hill is our home base, but we arrange private jet charter from Farnborough, Luton and Stansted, and from any suitable airport across the UK, Europe and worldwide — whichever is fastest and most convenient for you.

How quickly can you arrange a private jet from London?+

Our operations desk runs 24 hours a day. Private jet charter can often be arranged at short notice, including same-day departures, subject to aircraft and crew availability. For the best choice of aircraft and pricing, the earlier you enquire the better.

What size of private jet can I charter?+

A full range — from cost-effective turboprops (King Air 250, Pilatus PC-12) and light jets (Citation CJ1, M2, CJ2+, Phenom 300E), through midsize jets (Citation XLS+, Sovereign+), to our ultra-long-range specialism (Gulfstream G650, Global 7500, 6000 and Express XRS). We match the aircraft to your route, passengers and budget.

Do you operate the aircraft, or broker them?+

Both. 8Aviation flies operated and managed aircraft, and also brokers flights from a carefully selected group of operators we have worked with for many years. That means you get the right aircraft for the mission, from a trusted circle — not an open marketplace.

Is 8Aviation available 24 hours a day?+

Yes. Our operations team answers around the clock — for quotes, changes and last-minute departures — every day of the year.
